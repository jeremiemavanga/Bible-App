# Current visual direction — paper, ink and orange

The user's October 30, 2026 image reference supersedes the color and typography portions of `VISUAL-SPECIFICATION.md`. Functional scope, privacy, licensing and five-destination navigation remain unchanged. This revision reproduces the visual language, not the reference photograph or the pictured date.

- Warm paper background (`#E5E2DC`), ink text (`#20211F`), neutral gray surfaces and restrained orange (`#FF6B00`). Orange marks today and active navigation; primary actions are ink with paper-colored text.
- Oversized sans-serif day number, uppercase month, quiet year and weekday. Date and greeting follow the device's local timezone and refresh while the app is open; the sample February 2025 date is not hardcoded.
- A Monday-first, full-month circle calendar, including adjacent-month outline circles. Dark circles represent real recorded review attempts. Today has an orange fill, its day number and an outline. No fictitious completed days or punitive streaks are shown. Accessible date/review labels accompany every circle.
- System sans-serif throughout, strong editorial headings, flat reading surfaces, thin separators, restrained controls and a compact bottom navigation on iPhone. Desktop adapts the date/calendar and Scripture into a spacious split layout.
- The reader, memory exercises, Chapter Code, meditation, progress, persistent audio player, forms and backup controls inherit the same light design tokens. Focus indicators, contrast, reduced motion and 44px targets remain in place.

No third-party photo or font asset is bundled from the reference. The app's original installation icons were recolored to match. Historical design specifications and screenshots remain in Git history for provenance; this file defines the current direction.

## Scripture experience refinement

The agreed UX revision keeps the palette and circular calendar but gives Scripture and its primary action priority over the date. The date is smaller and the home calendar is expandable. The reader opens in continuous layout with search and preferences available in disclosures. See `SCRIPTURE-EXPERIENCE.md` for the accompanying practice and retention changes.

## Automatic practice refinement

The visual tokens and five primary destinations remain.
